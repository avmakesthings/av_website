Senior Product Design Lead, Meta, Horizon OS · Board Secretary, Gray Area Foundation

Hi, I'm Anastasia Victor, a Senior Design Lead with over a decade of experience designing products at the intersection of emerging technology, consumer hardware, and human experience. I am currently a product design lead for Horizon OS (our VR operating system) at Meta Reality Labs, where I focus on shaping intuitive, scalable interfaces for spatial computing.

My background spans architecture, UI/UX, and immersive systems, which gives me a unique lens on designing for people across physical and virtual environments. I'm especially drawn to 0→1 products—identifying unmet user needs, defining bold product visions, and leading multidisciplinary design teams to bring them to life.

I strongly believe virtual and augmented reality represent the next major paradigm of computing, and I actively use AI as a core part of my design and productivity workflows, from rapid prototyping to systems thinking and iteration.

Alongside my product work, I maintain an art practice focused on computational design and large-scale immersive installations, exploring movement, perception, and the body in space. I also serve on the Board of Directors for the Gray Area Foundation in San Francisco, supporting artists working at the edge of art, technology, and culture.
